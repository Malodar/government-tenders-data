The dataset compiles notices from official procurement portals. Each row names its source in `source` and that
source's reuse licence in `licence`. The compilation itself is licensed CC BY 4.0 (`data/LICENSE`).

- **TED (Tenders Electronic Daily)** (CC BY 4.0): Contains information from TED, © European Union, licensed under CC BY 4.0. https://ted.europa.eu

- **SAM.gov Contract Opportunities** (US government work (public domain)): Source: SAM.gov Contract Opportunities data extract, U.S. General Services Administration. https://sam.gov
- **BOAMP** (Licence Ouverte 2.0 (Etalab)): Source: BOAMP, DILA (Direction de l'information légale et administrative), Licence Ouverte 2.0. https://www.boamp.fr
- **Find a Tender** (Open Government Licence v3.0): Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk
- **AusTender** (CC BY 3.0 AU): Source: AusTender, © Commonwealth of Australia (Department of Finance), CC BY 3.0 AU. https://www.tenders.gov.au
- **Contracts Finder** (Open Government Licence v3.0): Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk
- **Kerndaten (data.gv.at)** (CC BY 4.0): Source: Kerndaten der Vergabeverfahren via data.gv.at, CC BY 4.0. https://www.data.gv.at

Values in euro use the European Central Bank's euro foreign exchange reference rates.

The data was normalised and partly modified (contact details removed, values converted, ids added). It is not an
official publication of any of these bodies, and none of them endorses it.
